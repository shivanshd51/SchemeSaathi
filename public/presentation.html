The Problem Statement

The Last-Mile Welfare Paradox in India

Thousands of crores in scholarships and youth welfare subsidies lapse every year. The root cause is not lack of intent—it is severe informational friction.

01

Fragmented Portals

Every department runs isolated websites with dead links, complex 40-page gazette PDFs, and outdated rules that confuse first-time applicants.

80% drop-off at discovery
02

Predatory Middlemen

Cyber-cafe operators charge ₹300–₹1,000 from low-income students just to fill basic free government forms, often using incorrect applicant emails.

High financial burden
03

Document Ambiguity

Students do not know the difference between mandatory certificates (Domicile, NPCI Bank Seeding) and conditional items, leading to form rejections.

Preventable rejections

Universal Location Engine (Any State & District)

Moving beyond single-city demos to support all 28 Indian States, 8 Union Territories, and 700+ districts.
